## Architecture Fit and Next Gates

PostgreSQL owns taxonomy IDs, versions, active/deprecated/replacement state; exact domain-ID matches are resolved there without requiring OAK. OAK applies to external ontology terms/relations and returns candidates only. Grounded AST/LSP or extraction evidence supplies source spans and revisions. Neo4j community/PageRank is a derived projection whose run/configuration/node map must be bound before resolving results to packets. Feature/domain proposals remain review-only until exact canonical identity and evidence admission pass. DSPy/GEPA should run only after a reviewed train/eval split exists; LangChain/Deep Agents remain optional orchestration, not taxonomy or persistence owners. Pydantic is a strict transport boundary, not semantic proof.
