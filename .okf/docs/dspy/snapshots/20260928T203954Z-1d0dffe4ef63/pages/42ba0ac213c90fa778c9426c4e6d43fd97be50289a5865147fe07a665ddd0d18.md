# DSPy in Production ¶
DSPy is in production at
Shopify, Databricks, Dropbox, Microsoft AI, Moody's, AWS, Sephora, VMware, Nubank
, and dozens more.
This list represents companies that have publicly shared their use cases or have provided permission to be included. It reflects a selection of the many industry applications of DSPy currently in production. To add yours, please
open a PR
.
