# OpenSpec Evidence Report Audit Prompt — 2026-10-03

Audit and organize the generated OpenSpec evidence reports before adding more of them to `main`.

## Objective

Determine which reports are durable, reproducible repository artifacts and which are raw or oversized run output. Produce a defensible disposition without deleting evidence or promoting unverified data.

## Scope

- `docs/reports/openspec-evidence/`
- Related generated OpenSpec reports under `docs/reports/`
- `openspec/changes/parent-atlas-openspec-tasks-audit-fabric/tasks.md`

## Required sequence

1. Inventory paths, byte sizes, checksums, timestamps, run IDs, and report relationships.
2. Classify every artifact as `PROMOTE`, `ARCHIVE`, `RETAIN_LOCAL`, or `REGENERATE`.
3. Review report content and provenance; do not use filename, timestamp, or apparent completeness as authority.
4. Keep raw evidence intact. Archive rather than delete, and do not move files until the disposition manifest is written.
5. Promote only curated summaries/manifests that are reproducible, useful to future audits, and under 10 MB per file.
6. Add narrowly scoped ignore rules only for repeat-generated raw outputs after checking tracked-file impact.
7. Validate the exact staged scope, inspect every staged path, audit filesystem and committed blob sizes, commit with a focused summary, and push only after remote alignment is proven.

## Hard constraints

- No Postgres, Redis, Qdrant, Neo4j, SeaweedFS, or production writes.
- No fabricated revisions, IDs, receipts, provenance, or completion claims.
- No deletion of evidence.
- No committed file over 10 MB.
- Do not stage the entire evidence tree for convenience.
- Keep incomplete or unproven live paths fail-closed.

## Deliverables

- A disposition manifest with path, checksum, byte size, provenance, classification, reason, and destination.
- A dated Markdown summary describing promoted, archived, retained, and regenerated artifacts.
- Updated OpenSpec task checkboxes with exact validation evidence.
